Munshi Group represents a Board of director’s dream coming to life.

Sharif Mahmud is a respected Islamic scholar, visionary entrepreneur, and the founder of Munshi Delivery Services, Munshi Delivery Services, a Qatar-based company. He was born in 1984 in Muradnagar, Cumilla, Bangladesh. In his expatriate life, he serves as a distinguished professional under the Ministry of Awqaf and Islamic Affairs in Qatar.

With the noble objective of creating employment opportunities for Bangladeshi expatriates and helping them achieve financial independence, he established Munshi Delivery Services. His vision is to strengthen the economy of Bangladesh by encouraging greater remittance inflows and to contribute towards building a poverty-free nation.

Beyond his professional endeavors, Sharif Mahmud is deeply inspired by the principles of Islam. He strives to lead his life in accordance with the teachings of the beloved Prophet Muhammad (peace and blessings be upon him), seeking the pleasure of Almighty Allah. Ethics, integrity, humanitarian service, and social responsibility form the foundation of his leadership.

Through his knowledge, wisdom, and strong humanitarian values, Munshi Delivery Services Delivery Services has grown into a trusted and reputable organization. He continues to be a source of inspiration for the Bangladeshi expatriate community.
